## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

In large-scale commercial enterprises, calculating the true profitability ("Cost of Deal") of enterprise merchant accounts is notoriously difficult. 

Before this initiative, three disconnected departments maintained conflicting numbers:
1. **Sales & Commercial:** Tracked promised contractual discount schedules in custom CRM databases.
2. **Operations & Ticketing:** Ingested high-velocity raw settlement records into Kafka and PostgreSQL datastores.
3. **Finance & Accounting:** Ran retrospective monthly Excel reconciliations that took over two weeks to close every calendar quarter.

The resulting metric drift caused disputes, delayed billing adjustments, and left executive decision-makers blind to margin leakage on high-volume enterprise contracts.

## 3. Key Technical Challenges & Solutions

### A. Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD Type 2) for Dynamic Rebates

Commercial fee schedules evolve over time as merchants hit volume milestones. To ensure financial auditability:
- We modeled commercial contracts as **SCD Type 2 tables** in BigQuery, retaining strict validity windows (`valid_from`, `valid_to`, `is_current`).
- Transactions are joined back to the fee schedule matching the exact millisecond the transaction occurred, ensuring historic numbers never fluctuate when a contract is renegotiated.

### B. Idempotency & Exactly-Once Ingestion

To guard against network retries in Kafka stream consumers:
- Each incoming transaction payload generates a deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint from `(transaction_id, account_id, timestamp_epoch)`.
- BigQuery `MERGE` statements execute with deduplication keys, ensuring replayable backfills with zero duplicate ledger entries.

```sql
MERGE \`financial_dw.fct_cost_of_deal\` T
USING \`staging.stg_incoming_transactions\` S
ON T.transaction_fingerprint = S.transaction_fingerprint
WHEN MATCHED AND S.update_timestamp > T.update_timestamp THEN
  UPDATE SET 
    gross_revenue = S.gross_revenue,
    rebate_accrual = S.rebate_accrual,
    net_margin = S.gross_revenue - S.rebate_accrual,
    updated_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()
WHEN NOT MATCHED THEN
  INSERT (transaction_fingerprint, account_id, gross_revenue, rebate_accrual, net_margin, created_at)
  VALUES (S.transaction_fingerprint, S.account_id, S.gross_revenue, S.rebate_accrual, S.gross_revenue - S.rebate_accrual, CURRENT_TIMESTAMP());
```
